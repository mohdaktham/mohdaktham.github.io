Model monitoring · Simulated data

Fraud Model Monitoring: Data Faults, Drift and New Fraud Patterns

A year of simulated card transactions with three planted events, and the monthly report that has to catch them. A gradient-boosted fraud model is checked every month for ranking power (AUC, KS), score and input shift (PSI, CSI), alert volume, and fraud cases and dollars caught, with fraud labels arriving two months late. The report told a broken data feed apart from a real change in fraud: the feed fault showed up as a 0.78 input shift, while the new fraud pattern moved no input at all and only appeared in outcomes once labels matured.
