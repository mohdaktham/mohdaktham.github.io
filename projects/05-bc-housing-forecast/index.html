BC Housing completions forecast

Monthly housing completions, forecast 36 months ahead for every regional district in British Columbia, with calibrated uncertainty bands and mortgage-rate scenarios, delivered as an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Consulting engagement with BC Housing through Wilfrid Laurier University, January to August 2026. My part was the data preparation, the forecasting models and the Power BI build. The results and the dashboard were presented to agency staff and programme leads.

Python, scikit-learn, Time series, Power BI, DAX

The question

How many homes will be completed in each BC regional district over the next 36 months, how wide is the uncertainty around that number, and how does it move if mortgage rates change?

What I did

  • Merged more than 36 years of CMHC housing data with economic indicators: mortgage rates, the policy rate and building permits.
  • Fitted one pooled Ridge regression across all districts, using lags of the target, lags of the under-construction pipeline, seasonal encodings and 12-month mortgage-rate changes with a 6-month lag.
  • Scaled each district using only past data (shifted trailing means), so districts of very different sizes share one model with no look-ahead leakage.
  • Built empirical 50, 80 and 95% prediction intervals from the backtest errors.
  • Added a scenario engine: the baseline against mortgage-rate paths 100 basis points higher and lower, with a table of forecast change per 100 basis points.
  • Cross-checked the forecasts against units physically under construction, using 18-month conversion ratios.
  • Wrote the outputs as eight data feeds for the Power BI dashboard, covering forecasts, scenarios, backtests, diagnostics and rate sensitivity.

What I found

  • 11.1% error against 20.7% for the naive benchmark on the BC total series, over 12 months held out of training.
  • The model beats naive on the large majority of the 24 regional series, not only on the provincial total.
  • The uncertainty bands hold up: 83% of held-out actuals landed inside the 80% band.

What is published

This is a write-up only. The forecasting script, its outputs and the model card belong to the client engagement and are not published, and the CMHC source workbook is not shared for data-sharing reasons.
