Executive KPI dashboard in Excel

An executive dashboard workbook for the Superstore retail dataset: six KPI cards and four native Excel charts, with no hardcoded numbers.

What it is

  • Dashboard sheet: 6 KPI cards (revenue, profit, margin, orders, average order value, best month) and 4 native Excel charts: monthly revenue trend, sales and profit by category, revenue by segment, and sales by region.
  • Data sheet: clean monthly, category, segment and region aggregates that feed every KPI.
  • Top Products sheet: a sortable, filterable sub-category table with live margin formulas.

Why it holds up

  • Every KPI is a live Excel formula referencing the Data sheet. Change the data and the whole dashboard recalculates.
  • Delivered with zero formula errors, confirmed by an automated recalculation of the workbook.
  • Follows finance formatting conventions: whole-dollar currency, one-decimal percentages and negatives in parentheses.

Headline numbers

Total revenue of $2.30M and profit of $286K, a 12.5% margin, across 5,009 orders. Technology is the top category and the West region leads sales.
